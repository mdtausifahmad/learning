Principal-Engineering Track

Technical Skills (Part A)

8 lessons · 8 reference sheets · 1 full Markdown guide•≈ 8–10 weeks part-time (3–5 hrs/week)

Everything is a trade-off: the Staff+ signal is naming the trade-off behind every technical choice and writing it down so a whole org can align. Each lesson is a single sitting, with quizzes and a “try it in your own system” task. Prefer one long read? The whole course is also in GUIDE.md (with Mermaid diagrams). See MISSION.md for the why.

For a senior engineer working toward Staff or Principal. These eight lessons cover A2–A9 of Part A of the skills playbook: the theory beneath system design, keeping systems up in production, architecture and strategy across teams, the writing that scales your judgement, security, cloud, and the shape of your expertise. A1 — system design itself — is its own course: System Design & Architecture at Scale. Every lesson ends with quizzes, a try-it list for your own system and a primary source, so you finish able to name, defend and write down the trade-off behind each choice.

Part I · Distributed foundations

The theory beneath system design, and how to keep it dependable in production.

  1. A2Data-Intensive & Distributed Systems
    Schema evolution, partial failure, clocks and causality, consensus vs 2PC, batch vs stream.
  2. A3Reliability, Observability & SRE
    SLOs and error budgets, golden signals, toil, incidents and postmortems, overload, safe rollouts.

Part II · Architecture & direction

Zoom out from one system to styles, multi-team strategy and the writing that carries it.

  1. A4Software Architecture & Evolutionary Design
    Styles, coupling, data ownership, sagas, Strangler Fig migrations, fitness functions, tech debt.
  2. A5Engineering Strategy & Technical Vision
    Rumelt's kernel, strategy vs vision, the five-design-docs recipe, guiding policies, enforcement.
  3. A6Technical Writing — Design Docs, RFCs & ADRs
    Design docs, RFCs and ADRs: when to write which, what goes in each, and healthy review culture.

Part III · Cross-cutting concerns

Security, privacy and the cloud platform every design runs on.

  1. A7Security & Privacy as a Design Concern
    OWASP Top 10, authN vs authZ, secure APIs, core principles, crypto basics, STRIDE and PII.
  2. A8Cloud Infrastructure & Pragmatic Tech Choices
    Scaling progression, primitives, Kubernetes trade-offs, IaC, deploys, RTO/RPO, FinOps, boring tech.

Part IV · Shape of expertise

Turn A1–A8 into broad judgement plus one deep, business-critical spike.

  1. A9T-shaped Breadth + Depth
    Breadth for judgement, a business-critical spike for authority, and a quarterly loop to build both.
