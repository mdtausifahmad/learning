# Course delivered: Technical Skills (Part A), Senior → Staff/Principal

The learner (senior → Staff/Principal) has worked through Part A of the roadmap in
[Skills to upgrade](../../02-skills-to-upgrade.md) as eight markdown chapters. These were converted into the
same course format as the sibling topics:

- [GUIDE.md](../GUIDE.md): the long-form markdown edition with Mermaid diagrams, organised in four parts
  (distributed foundations; architecture & direction; cross-cutting concerns; shape of expertise), with
  a self-assessment, a study plan and a capstone.
- [index.html](../index.html): 8 HTML lessons (Lesson N = roadmap item A(N+1)). Each has quizzes, a
  "try it" block, a primary source and a staff-level mission tie. There are also 8 print-friendly
  [reference cheat sheets](../reference/0001-data-intensive-distributed-systems-cheatsheet.html).
- [MISSION.md](../MISSION.md), [NOTES.md](../NOTES.md), [GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) and
  [RESOURCES.md](../RESOURCES.md): the course contract, preferences, canonical terms and curated sources.

A1 (system design at scale) remains its own course: [System Design](../../system-design/index.html).
Lesson 02 points to [Observability](../../Observability/index.html) for the hands-on deep dive, and
Lesson 05 pairs with [Leadership Skills](../../leadership-skills/index.html).

Baseline assumption: strong senior builder; the gap is multi-team judgment and writing. Natural next step:
take the self-assessment in `GUIDE.md`, then start the lesson that covers your weakest area. Lessons 03
and 05 are the usual high-leverage starting points. The capstone design-review package is the exit criterion.
