# Notes & Preferences

## Learner profile
- **Senior** engineer targeting **Staff/Principal**. Part of the broader plan in
  [Skills to upgrade](../02-skills-to-upgrade.md) (Part A, technical skills A2–A9).
- Already builds and operates production services. The gap is **multi-team judgment**: naming
  guarantees and trade-offs, setting direction, and scaling that judgment through writing.
- A1 (system design) is a separate course, [System Design](../system-design/index.html). Take it first or
  alongside; Lessons 01, 02 and 07 lean on it.

## Teaching preferences
- Visual and interactive: HTML lessons ([index.html](index.html)) with diagrams, quizzes, a "try it" block
  and a staff-level mission tie. They use the same Tufte-style format and shared assets (`/assets/course/`)
  as the sibling courses.
- [GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md) is the long-form markdown edition with Mermaid diagrams. Use it to read end to
  end or to search.
- Every lesson ends with **staff-level judgment**: trade-offs, failure modes, review questions and the
  traps that show up in real design reviews.
- Every lesson has a print-friendly **cheat sheet** in `reference/`, for example
  [Reliability & SRE](reference/0002-reliability-observability-sre-cheatsheet.html).
- Ground exercises in the learner's **own systems and documents**, not toy examples.

## Conventions
- Course identity colour: **bronze** (#8a5a12), set in `assets/theme.css`. Siblings:
  [system-design](../system-design/index.html) cobalt (#1f4f8f), event-sourcing indigo (#4b3f8f),
  [Observability](../Observability/index.html) teal (#14615f), async-messaging brick (#7a2e2e),
  [leadership-skills](../leadership-skills/index.html) plum (#6d2e6b).
- Lesson N covers roadmap item A(N+1): Lesson 01 = A2 … Lesson 08 = A9.
- Quizzes: scenario-based, 4 options of similar length, per-option feedback, explanation after answering.
- Terms follow [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md). Sources are in [RESOURCES.md](RESOURCES.md).
