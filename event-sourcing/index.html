Principal-Engineering Track

Event Sourcing: Level 0 → Staff

16 lessons · 12 reference sheets · 5 interactive simulators · 1 runnable lab•Java 21 · PostgreSQL · Kafka · Podman

From “what is an event?” to deciding — and defending in an architecture review — whether, where and how your organisation should event-source a domain. Each lesson is a single sitting, with an animated walkthrough, quizzes, and a “try it in your system” task. Prefer one long read? The whole course is also in GUIDE.md (with Mermaid diagrams). See MISSION.md for the why.

Part I · Foundations (Level 0–1)

Store facts, derive state.

  1. State vs history: the mental modelsim
    The inversion; fold; why (and why not).
  2. Events, commands & designing good events
    Past tense, intent over diffs, envelopes, correlation/causation.
  3. The Decider: aggregates in Java 21
    Pure decide/evolve, sealed types, minimal state.
  4. The event store & optimistic concurrencysim
    Postgres schema, expectedVersion, stream design.

Part II · The read side (Level 2)

CQRS: answer any question, eventually.

  1. CQRS & projectionssim
    Idempotent, checkpointed, rebuildable.
  2. Rebuilds & living with eventual consistency
    Blue/green rebuilds; read-your-writes; UX strategies.

Part III · Production engineering (Level 3)

What separates a demo from a system you can page someone about.

  1. Snapshots & closing the books
    Caches, not truth; short-lived streams.
  2. Subscriptions & the global-ordering gapsim
    Catch-up vs persistent; the bug that skips events forever.
  3. Publishing: integration events & Kafka
    Domain vs integration events; no dual writes; is Kafka an event store?
  4. Sagas, process managers & cross-aggregate invariants
    Compensation, timeouts, uniqueness, DCB.
  5. Choosing an event store & operating it
    Postgres vs KurrentDB vs Axon vs DynamoDB; SLOs & observability.

Part IV · Advanced (Level 4)

Events are forever — make them evolvable, lawful and testable.

  1. Event schema evolution & upcastingsim
    Weak schema, upcasters, new types, copy-transform.
  2. GDPR, crypto-shredding & temporal queries
    Forgetting in an immutable log; bi-temporality.
  3. Testing event-sourced systems
    Given/When/Then, property tests, replay tests.
  4. Migrating from CRUD & Event Storming
    Strangler path; discovering events with the business.

Part V · Staff judgement (Level 5)

The decision, the trade-offs, and the people.

  1. When (not) to event-source, anti-patterns & the ADR · capstone
    Decision framework, design review, org adoption, the Ledgerly capstone.
