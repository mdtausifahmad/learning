The one equation

state = events.foldLeft(initial, evolve)  ·  newEvents = decide(command, state). Events are the truth; state, snapshots and read models are caches.

Common confusions

ES ≠ Kafka (no per-entity streams with OCC). Snapshots and read models are never the source of truth. Fix mistakes with compensating events — never UPDATE/DELETE an event.
