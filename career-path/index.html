From Senior to Staff to Principal

A career-development path for understanding the next level of impact, deciding what to work on, and making your growth visible.

Read the seven parts in order, then revisit the plan and tracking tools regularly. This path draws on Staff+ literature, public engineering ladders and first-person promotion stories; its career guide brings the context and sources together.

The career playbook

  1. What is required from a Staff / Principal engineer
    Expectations, archetypes and the gap in scope and impact.
  2. Skills to upgrade
    Technical and leadership skills, why they matter, and what good looks like.
  3. Career progression plan
    A phased plan combining learning, leadership and evidence of growth.
  4. Subjects and resources
    A sequenced curriculum for this career path.
  5. Promotion playbook
    Sponsorship, promotion packets, meaningful projects and the committee process.
  6. Self-assessment and tracking
    Score your skills, keep a brag document and track milestones.
  7. Real-world stories and lessons
    First-person accounts of growing into Staff and Principal roles.

Build skills alongside the path

Use System Design for technical trade-offs, Technical Skills for wider engineering judgment, and Leadership Skills for influence and collaboration.
