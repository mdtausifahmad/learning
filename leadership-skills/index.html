Principal-Engineering Track

Leadership Skills (Part B)

8 lessons · 8 reference sheets · 1 full Markdown guide•≈ 8 weeks part-time, plus a 90-day capstone you run in your day job

At Staff+, authority shrinks as scope grows: you lead through influence, communication and force-multiplication, not command. Each lesson is a single sitting, with quizzes and a “try it in your own system” task. Prefer one long read? The whole course is also in GUIDE.md (with Mermaid diagrams). See MISSION.md for the why.

For Senior engineers aiming at Staff or Principal who already have the technical chops and now need the skills the promotion is actually won on. Eight lessons cover Part B of Skills to upgrade: influence, executive communication, project leadership, sponsorship, trust, glue work, decision-making and prioritization. Each one ends with scenario quizzes and actions to run at work this week, and the evidence they produce feeds your brag doc and your promotion packet.

Part I · Influence & communication

The master skill and how it travels: questions, purpose, SCQA, the pyramid and nemawashi.

  1. B1Influence Without Authority
    Borrowed authority: ask before you assert, name the purpose, read the room, and follow quickly.
  2. B2Communication & Executive Writing
    Make messages land with executives: SCQA openings, Minto Pyramid, nemawashi and answer-first memos.

Part II · Leading through others

Apply influence to delivery, then multiply it through delegation and sponsorship.

  1. B3Technical & Project Leadership
    Lead ambiguous multi-team work: scope it, delegate the hard parts, map the shadow org, land it.
  2. B4Mentorship & Sponsorship
    Advice versus opportunity: sponsor people into visible work, scaffold them, let the credit stay theirs.

Part III · Capital & visibility

Earn and spend borrowed authority, and make the invisible work count.

  1. B5Stakeholder Management, Trust & Political Capital
    Proxied authority: never surprise your manager, bank political capital, build peers before you need them.
  2. B6Glue Work — Made Visible
    Name, scope, measure, rotate and reward the coordination work that holds teams together.

Part IV · Judgement at scale

Decide, disagree well, and choose where your scarce attention goes.

  1. B7Decision-Making & Disagreeing Productively
    Transfer judgement: write reasoning, circulate early, split style from substance, judge decisions not luck.
  2. B8Prioritization at Scale — "Work on What Matters"
    Leverage is what you choose: skip snacks, preening and ghosts, and invest in existential issues first.
