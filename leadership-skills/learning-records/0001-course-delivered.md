# Course delivered: Leadership Skills (Part B), Senior → Staff/Principal

The learner (senior → Staff/Principal, technically strong) needs to turn leadership into deliberate practice and promotion evidence. The topic started as eight markdown deep dives (B1–B8, Part B of `../../02-skills-to-upgrade.md`). It was converted to the same course format as `../../event-sourcing/`:

- [`GUIDE.md`](../GUIDE.md): the long-form markdown edition, with chapters B1–B8 merged in order and their Mermaid diagrams kept.
- [`index.html`](../index.html): the course map. Eight lessons in four parts:
  - **Influence & communication** (01–02)
  - **Leading through others** (03–04)
  - **Capital & visibility** (05–06)
  - **Judgement at scale** (07–08)

  It also holds a self-assessment, an 8-week study plan and a 90-day leadership-plan capstone.
- `lessons/0001…0008-*.html`: one lesson per chapter (lesson N = BN). Each ends with:
  - a mission tie;
  - three scenario quizzes (realistic workplace judgement calls with per-option feedback);
  - try-it actions for this week at work;
  - a primary source and a follow-up prompt.
- `reference/0001…0008-*-cheatsheet.html`: print-friendly sheets with scripts and phrases, checklists, decision tables and traps. For example, [Influence without authority](../reference/0001-influence-without-authority-cheatsheet.html) and [Prioritization](../reference/0008-prioritization-cheatsheet.html).
- [`MISSION.md`](../MISSION.md), [`NOTES.md`](../NOTES.md), [`GLOSSARY.md`](../GLOSSARY.md) (45 canonical terms), [`RESOURCES.md`](../RESOURCES.md) (every chapter's further reading).

Course identity colour: plum (#6d2e6b). Companion course: [Technical Skills](../../technical-skills/index.html).

Baseline assumption: strong technical execution; leadership happens *ad hoc* and mostly goes unrecorded.

Natural next steps:
1. Lesson 01, starting the two-week influence log.
2. Start the capstone: a 90-day leadership plan. Pick one existential or high-leverage initiative, a sponsor, two people to sponsor and a weekly update cadence.
3. Log the evidence in the brag doc ([`../../06-self-assessment-and-tracking.md`](../../06-self-assessment-and-tracking.md)) and frame it with STAR-E for the packet ([`../../05-promotion-playbook.md`](../../05-promotion-playbook.md)).
