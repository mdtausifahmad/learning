# Notes & Preferences

## Learner profile
- **Senior** engineer targeting **Staff / Principal**. This is part of the broader plan in `../README.md` and Part B of `../02-skills-to-upgrade.md`.
- Technically strong; delivery and architecture are not the gap. The gap is **leadership practice**: influence across teams, executive communication, sponsorship, trust and prioritization. These must show up as evidence a promotion committee can see.
- No direct reports. Works through borrowed authority: a manager or sponsor, peers, and cross-functional partners.

## Teaching preferences
- HTML lessons (`lessons/000N-*.html`) use the shared course assets in `/assets/course/` (course.css, quiz/links/mermaid scripts) plus this course's `assets/theme.css`. Each lesson ends with:
  - a mission tie;
  - three **scenario quizzes** set in realistic workplace situations;
  - **try-it actions for this week at work**;
  - a primary source and a follow-up prompt.
- `GUIDE.md` is the long-form markdown edition (chapters B1–B8 merged, with Mermaid diagrams). Use it for reading end to end or for search.
- Reference sheets (`reference/000N-*-cheatsheet.html`) are print-friendly compressions: scripts and phrases, checklists, decision tables and traps. Keep one open before a hard meeting.
- Practice beats reading. Tie every exercise to real work and log the result in the brag doc / sponsorship journal from `../06-self-assessment-and-tracking.md`. Frame outcomes in packet language (STAR-E) from `../05-promotion-playbook.md`.
- Every lesson ends with **Staff-level judgement**: when to use a move, when *not* to, and what it costs in political capital.

- Course identity colour: **plum** (#6d2e6b). Sibling courses use:
  - system-design: cobalt (#1f4f8f)
  - technical-skills: bronze (#8a5a12)
  - event-sourcing: indigo (#4b3f8f)
  - Observability: teal
  - async-messaging: brick
- Eyebrow labels: "Leadership Skills · Lesson 0N" and "Leadership Skills · Reference 0N". Lesson N corresponds to chapter BN of Part B.
- Course parts:
  - Influence & communication (01–02)
  - Leading through others (03–04)
  - Capital & visibility (05–06)
  - Judgement at scale (07–08)
- Quizzes: 3 per lesson, 4 options of similar length, exactly one correct, per-option feedback, explanation after answering.
- [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md) is canonical; lessons and reference sheets use its terms exactly.
- Markdown is rendered by Jekyll. Never write Liquid braces (double curly braces or brace-percent) in any `.md` file.
- Related course: [Technical Skills](../technical-skills/index.html) covers Part A (the technical track).
