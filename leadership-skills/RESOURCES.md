# Leadership Skills — Resources

Curated, high-trust sources: the "Further reading" from every chapter, plus the books they cite. Verify the links before relying on them; blogs move.

## Canonical: StaffEng guides
- [Operating at Staff](https://staffeng.com/guides/operating-at-staff/): the overview of what Staff+ work looks like day to day. Start here.
- [Learn to never be wrong](https://staffeng.com/guides/learn-to-never-be-wrong/): the three moves (questions, purpose, room) and handling difficult people before the meeting. Lessons 01 and 07.
- [To lead, you have to follow](https://staffeng.com/guides/to-lead-follow/): choosing battles and backing trustworthy leaders. Lesson 01.
- [Present to executives](https://staffeng.com/guides/present-to-executives/): communication jobs, SCQA and the rules for executive rooms. Lesson 02.
- [What do Staff engineers actually do?](https://staffeng.com/guides/what-do-staff-engineers-actually-do/): setting direction, mentorship, sponsorship and glue. Lessons 03–05.
- [Manage technical quality](https://staffeng.com/guides/manage-technical-quality/): the escalating quality toolkit, from hot spots to a funded program. Lesson 03.
- [Create space for others](https://staffeng.com/guides/create-space-for-others/): delegation, transferring judgement and not filling every space. Lessons 03, 04 and 07.
- [Staying aligned with authority](https://staffeng.com/guides/staying-aligned-with-authority/): proxied authority and never surprising your manager. Lesson 05.
- [Build a network of peers](https://staffeng.com/guides/network-of-peers/): why the work gets lonelier and how to fix it before a crisis. Lesson 05.
- [Work on what matters](https://staffeng.com/guides/work-on-what-matters/): snacking, preening, chasing ghosts, existential issues. Lessons 06 and 08.

## Books
- Tanya Reilly, [*The Staff Engineer's Path*](https://noidea.dog/staff): big-picture thinking, project execution and levelling up others. It is the source of the passive/active influence and political-capital ideas.
- Barbara Minto, *The Pyramid Principle*: answer first, grouped and ordered arguments. The basis of executive writing in Lesson 02.
- Annie Duke, *Thinking in Bets*: decision quality vs outcome quality, and "resulting". Lesson 07.
- Patterson, Grenny, McMillan & Switzler, *Crucial Conversations*: making it safe to talk about high-stakes disagreement. Lesson 07.
- Chris Voss, *Never Split the Difference*: calibrated questions and tactical empathy. Lesson 07.

## Essays & practitioners
- Tanya Reilly, [*Being Glue*](https://www.noidea.dog/glue): the talk and essay that named glue work and its career risk. Lesson 06.
- Lara Hogan, [*What does sponsorship look like?*](https://larahogan.me/blog/what-sponsorship-looks-like/): a concrete menu of sponsorship actions anyone can take. Lesson 04.
- Harvard Business Review, [*Women Are Over-Mentored (but Under-Sponsored)*](https://hbr.org/2010/08/women-are-over-mentored-but-un): the evidence behind the over-mentored / under-sponsored pattern. Lesson 04.
- Malte Ubl, [*Design Docs at Google*](https://www.industrialempathy.com/posts/design-docs-at-google/): how written design docs scale reasoning and decisions. Lessons 02 and 07.
- The Pragmatic Engineer, [*The Staff Engineer's Path*](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-staff-engineers-path): an industry view of Staff+ expectations and influence. Lessons 01, 03 and 08.
- Will Larson, [*The forty year career*](https://lethain.com/forty-year-career/): sustainable pacing and choosing roles for learning. Lesson 08.
- [Amazon Leadership Principles](https://www.amazon.jobs/content/en/our-workplace/leadership-principles): the canonical statement of "Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit". Lesson 07.

## Community
- [Rands Leadership Slack](https://randsinrepose.com/welcome-to-rands-leadership-slack/): an external peer network where engineering leaders trade practical advice. Lesson 05.
