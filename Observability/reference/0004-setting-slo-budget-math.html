Reliability & SRE · Reference 04

Setting an SLO & Budget Math

Compressed from Lesson 03•Print-friendly

The method

  1. Measure your SLI over ~4 weeks.
  2. Round to a starter target: availability down to 2 significant figures; latency thresholds up to the nearest ~50 ms.
  3. Pick the window — default to a 4-week rolling window.
  4. Compute the error budget in events.
  5. Write the SLO doc & get sign-off from product, devs, and ops/SRE.
Current-performance trap

Measured performance is a fine start if you iterate — but users come to expect whatever you deliver, so don’t enshrine a too-tight number you’ll have to defend forever. Aim at “above this, almost all users are happy.”

Choosing the window

Rolling (e.g. 4 weeks)Calendar (month / quarter)
Best forthe decision-driving SLObusiness reporting & SLAs
Whytracks recent experience; no resetaligns with planning cycles
Watch out—Jan-1 reset gaming; mid-period traffic unknown

Use whole weeks so weekend counts match. Complement with weekly (course-correct) and quarterly (planning) summaries.

Budget math

error budget (events) = (1 − SLO) × valid events in the window
budget remaining      = budget − failures so far
an outage costs        (its failed events) / (budget)  of your window

99.9% SLO → budget by volume

100k req100 failures

Budget as time (4-week window)

99%6.7 hours
99.9%40 minutes
99.95%20 minutes
99.99%4 minutes

Always state the window with the budget: 99.9% is ~40 min over 4 weeks, ~43 min over 30 days.

Spent · remaining · burn

  • Spent = failures so far. Remaining = budget − spent.
  • Burn rate = spend speed vs even pace. 1× empties the budget exactly at window end; 10× empties it in a tenth of the window.
  • You alert on burn rate, not on every blip (Lesson 04).

SLO document & sign-off

  • SLI specification and implementation
  • Target + window + rationale
  • Error-budget policy (what happens at zero — Lesson 04)

Three signatures: Product (target good enough for users) · Developers (will reduce risk when budget is spent) · Ops/SRE (defensible without heroics or burnout). The negotiation is the value.
